## Shot list

1. **The question at dawn** — sunrise over a palace garden courtyard; Fan-di taps his closed fan against his palm over a hard question, Dr-Qi steps in with an open bamboo scroll, Lei-Gong skids in late at the frame edge. (Maps to Script Scenes 1–2.)
2. **The four-season calendar** — Dr-Qi in the lower foreground holds her scroll like a tablet as a luminous wheel-of-the-seasons — spring blossom, summer lotus, autumn leaf, winter snow — turns behind her. (Script Scene 2.)
3. **Spring & Summer** — a vertical split: top, Lei-Gong stretching loose-haired amid cherry blossoms; bottom, summer heat-shimmer where Fan-di fans himself in shade and a sulking Lei-Gong refuses the sun. (Script Scenes 3–4: Liver / Heart.)
4. **Autumn & Winter** — top, golden fields and falling leaves with Dr-Qi walking briskly at first light; bottom, a snow-locked palace where she banks a fire early as a worn-down Lei-Gong finally listens. (Script Scenes 5–6: Lungs / Kidneys.)
5. **The cascade / heaven's warning** — the season-wheel spins and smears into storm clouds that gather and refuse to break; ancient trees blacken and the sun dims while Dr-Qi and Fan-di watch the horizon, small and uneasy. (Script Scenes 7–8.)
6. **The sage's secret / title card** — night candlelight, the three sitting close and intimate; the frame settles into a series title-card layout, season motifs arced above, clean lower third held for the title overlay. (Script Scenes 9–10.)
