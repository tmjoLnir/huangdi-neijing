## Shot list

1. **The fear** — night interior of a village room, hearth glow; Lei-Gong sits doubled over on a mat, one hand to his stomach, his little hand drum fallen beside him. Behind him a tall pale grey **smoke silhouette** of a spirit-healer rises out of the hearth smoke, faceless, shaking a ritual drum in slow motion. Moody and atmospheric, never horror. Slow push in. (Source Beat 1.)
2. **The turn** — the smoke silhouette freezes mid-gesture and thins away to nothing, dismissed. Dr-Qi steps in calmly from the right, kneels, and lays two fingers on Lei-Gong's **wrist**. A single soft pulse of warm light blooms at the wrist, once, then again, steady. The palette warms from smoky grey to clean daylight. (Source Beat 2.)
3. **Storehouses & rivers** — a graphic montage, no people: a row of five sealed round **storehouse vessels** each filling with a steady inner glow and holding it, lids closed; hard cut to a row of **open channels** like ink rivers, gates swinging open in sequence as light rushes through and empties out the far end; then a small hybrid cluster, half vessel and half channel, glowing quietly between the two. (Source Beat 3, first half — 藏而不泻 / 泻而不藏 and the 奇恒之腑.)
4. **One window** — a luminous wave travels down a stylised ink chart of a torso, from a vessel at the top through the winding channels below — the journey of a single meal — then leaps outward and lands as **one bright point on an outstretched wrist** in the lower third. Fan-di watches from the frame edge and slowly lowers his fan closed, genuinely caught. (Source Beat 3, second half.)
5. **The line in the ground** — near-black, deep ink-wash. A closed bamboo scroll with thick grey smoke settling over it, refusing to lift, so the scroll cannot be opened; a heavy **cinnabar brush stroke** sweeps beneath it. Hard cut: a shut wooden door with warm light pressing at its edges and unable to pass; a second cinnabar stroke sweeps beneath. Two deep slow beats. (Source Beat 4 — the two manifesto lines, rendered as image rather than lettering.)
6. **Payoff / title card** — the three characters resolve from flat black silhouette into full colour, the chapter motif of sealed vessels and open rivers arcing overhead with one warm point of light at a wrist. The lower third is kept clean and empty for the title overlay. (Source Beat 5.)

Subtitles/CC are burned in server-side at assembly (font **anton**), per the
project's compliance rule to always caption the deliverable; the clips
themselves stay text-free.

## Deliverables the assembler cannot produce

The source script carries two hard on-screen-text requirements that
`explainer_video` has no parameter for — its only text layer is Whisper-timed
subtitles transcribed from the voiceover. Both must be added as overlays at
upload/edit time, or the cut does not meet the script's own requirements:

1. **History lower-third** — *"Presented as history & philosophy"*, small, on
   screen inside the first 10 seconds (over block 1).
2. **Human editorial credit** — *"Written & edited by [HUMAN NAME]"* on the end
   card (over block 6) **and** in the video description, per the AI-persona
   policy.

The not-medical-advice disclaimer goes in the first two lines of the
description. Music is not generated by this pipeline: the script's
**licensed-guqin-only** requirement is a manual step at edit, and matters more
here than on the episode because a trailer earns disproportionate plays and so
carries higher Content-ID exposure.

## Compliance notes (YouTube)

- **Supernatural hook, debunked in-cut** — this is the load-bearing one for
  Chapter 8. The ghost is raised in block 1 and refused by block 2 ("refused the
  spell, and reached for the pulse"), with the payoff stated outright in block 6:
  "Not demons. Evidence." The hook is never left dangling and the title honors
  the educational payoff.
- **The smoke-healer is never glorified** — it stays a faceless pale grey
  silhouette, is visibly the worldview being *refused* (it freezes and dissolves
  the moment Dr-Qi enters), and it never speaks, never heals, and never wins.
- **Medical content** — philosophy/history framing only. No condition is named,
  no treatment is prescribed, no efficacy is claimed. The pulse is presented as
  what the text *reads*, not as a diagnostic instruction. Disclaimer carried as
  the blockquote above and in the description.
- **The needle line is cut.** The source script's third historical line —
  "Afraid of the needle — and you cannot learn its skill" — is flagged in the
  script itself as 1:30-only, to be used only with the history lower-third on
  screen. It appears nowhere in this 0:60 cut, verbally or visually, and there
  are no needles in any shot.
- **Mortality / illness** — "portraits, not bodies." Lei-Gong's illness is a boy
  doubled over with a hand on his stomach in one clip and fully recovered by the
  last; no injury, no bodies, no clinical depiction. The manifesto beat is
  objects and brush strokes, not people.
- **Indulgence** — no feast, no bottles, no pouring, no drinking.
- **Audience** — general audiences; self-certify **not** "made for kids."
