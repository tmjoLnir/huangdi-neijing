## Shot list

1. **The root** — black screen; a single stroke of ink blooms across wet rice paper in extreme slow motion and splits into two curling tails, which settle into a taiji circle rendered as *weather* — one half rolling cloud, one half falling rain. Fan-di fades up out of the ink in the lower third, fan closed, spectacles catching the light. (Maps to the Cold Open, 00:00–01:25.)
2. **The law before the medicine** — a palace hall with open screens, rain in the courtyard beyond; Fan-di paces at the left, Dr-Qi opens her bamboo scroll at the right. Pale motes rise and gather into cloud along the top of the frame while heavy dark motes settle into a band of land at the bottom — "pile up yang and you get sky; pile up yin and you get earth." (Act I.)
3. **The weather inside you** — aerial mist lifting off a river valley at dawn, rising into cloud, rain falling back; the valley match-cuts into a standing human silhouette, the mist becoming breath. Gold routing arrows run up and outward, dark arrows down and inward; then the arrows reverse and glow red — the clear trapped low, the turbid jammed high. Lei-Gong counts them off on his fingers. (Act II.)
4. **The fire that eats** — a single small oil lamp burning steady in a quiet night interior; hard cut to a bonfire roaring through a woodpile, Lei-Gong watching a little too close and delighted, satchel sparking; the bonfire collapses to grey ash and his sparks go dark; cut back to the oil lamp, unchanged, still burning. (Act III: 壮火食气.)
5. **The compass of five** — Dr-Qi unrolls the scroll and it keeps going down the tall frame; above it a five-pointed compass rose of brushed ink ignites one arm at a time — green, red, yellow, white, black — then reconfigures into a closed ring of five arrows, the controlling cycle. Beside it an ink-brush silhouette rigid with fury has its red glow drain downward as fine rain falls through it. Dr-Qi stands very still. (Act IV, the centrepiece.)
6. **The four ranks / title card** — a brushed diagram of a standing figure in five concentric layers illuminating from the outside inward, the innermost ring glowing red; extreme close-up of three fingers laid on a wrist, held long; then all three characters together, Fan-di closing his fan with a small final motion, the taiji weather circle arced above, settling into a title-card layout with a clean lower third. (Act VII and the Close: 见微得过 / 治病必求于本.)

## Compliance notes (YouTube)

- **Medical content** — philosophy/history framing only; no condition is paired
  with a treatment, no substance is named, and no efficacy is asserted. Every
  clinical-sounding line is reported speech about what the text claims. The
  not-medical-advice disclaimer is carried as the blockquote above and goes in
  the first two lines of the video description.
- **The honesty line is load-bearing** — block 6 opens "Many of its answers turned
  out wrong." The full script's compliance audit flags the Act IV / Close
  admissions that specific claims are false as required for the
  medical-misinformation policy; the trailer carries that admission rather than
  selling the correspondence table as fact. **Do not cut that line.**
- **Mortality / collapse** — kept atmospheric, "portraits, not bodies." The
  blazing-fire passage is a bonfire guttering to ash and a lamp that keeps
  burning; the four-ranks passage is an abstract five-layer diagram. No bodies,
  no injury, no depiction of the script's "half life, half death."
- **七损八益** — the seven-depletions/eight-replenishments material, the full
  script's amber-flag advertiser-suitability item, is **not used in the trailer at
  all**, verbally or visually.
- **Indulgence** — no feast, no bottles, no pouring, no drinking.
- **Supernatural hook** — "a claim about reality" (block 1) is explicitly defused
  in block 2 ("not mysticism — a set of operating rules") and paid off
  educationally in block 6. The title honors the payoff rather than the mystery.
- **Audience** — general audiences; self-certify **not** "made for kids."
