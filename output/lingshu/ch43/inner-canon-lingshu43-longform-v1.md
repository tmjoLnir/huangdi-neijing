## Why 16:00, and not 14:00 or 17:00

The requested window was 14–17 minutes with a hard floor of 11:30, and the brief asked
for the length that minimises padding and shortening. **96 blocks — 16:00 — is where
this chapter's usable material actually lands**, and the reasoning is arithmetic rather
than taste, because a 10s block is the unit that gets padded or cut.

This chapter's problem is the opposite of most on the slate: it is **short and almost
entirely usable**. Three sections, no digressions, no prognostic passage, and a
catalogue of **twenty-seven entries** — twelve of excess and fifteen of shortfall —
every one of which is a distinct image. The material that sets the length is not the
word count of the source but **the number of entries multiplied by the number of layers
the assigned structural form requires.** Costing it by what each part can carry without
inventing filler:

| Material | Blocks | Why that many |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open — the two universal dreams, the answer stated bare, the demand, citation, title, thesis, the auditor's brief | 8 | the frame, and the hook stated before anything is quoted |
| Act I — the mechanism, the title mistranslation, the two circulations, **the hun and po debunk**, the one dial | 12 | a viewer who thinks this is an occult text cannot hear the rest, and the debunk cannot wait |
| Act II — layer one: water and fire and mutual killing; the grammar of the catalogue exposed | 10 | the first class, and the one place the method is stated rather than shown |
| Act III — layer two: flying and falling, the modern universals, the hypnic jerk, **the falsifiability finding** | 12 | the hook material, and the only entry in the book that could have been checked |
| Act IV — layer three: taking and giving; **Artemidorus, the Zhou dream-diviner, Zhuangzi**; reckoning one | 12 | the comparative frame, which is what makes this intellectual history rather than a listicle |
| Act V — layer four: the five organ entries and the drain line | 12 | the chapter's most-quoted and most-misquoted material, and it needs its hedges attached |
| **Act VI — layer five: the fifteen lodgings, the anatomical route, the bladder incorporation** | 14 | fifteen entries plus the finding that explains why there are fifteen |
| Act VII — the true wake, both reckonings, Aristotle, Freud, activation-synthesis, thesis, end card | 16 | four rival dream theories scored honestly, and the reckoning cannot be a single line at 16:00 |

That totals **96**. Both edges of the requested window cost something real:

- **14:00 is 84 blocks — 12 short**, and there is no clean twelve anywhere. The only
  contiguous twelve that can go without breaking a layer of the nested frame is **most
  of Act IV** — Artemidorus, the Zhou dream-diviner, Zhuangzi and the taking-and-giving
  pair. **Cost:** every comparison the episode makes with another dream theory, which is
  the entire reason the material is intellectual history rather than a dream-decoder
  video. It is available and is specified exactly in *Runtime levers*, and it is the
  version to make if the runtime is forced down.
- **17:00 is 102 blocks — 6 too many, and the six are reachable but weaker.** Two of
  them are genuinely good and are named in *Runtime levers* as the 16:40 stretch: a
  fuller Artemidorus and the Zhuangzi butterfly given its own beat. **The remaining four
  are not.** The only material left is the two entries this series excludes on
  compliance grounds, a second pass through the catalogue, or a general segment on
  dreaming that the chapter does not support. **The honest answer is that 17:00 is
  reachable and that four of the six blocks that get it there are padding**, which is
  precisely what the brief asked to avoid.
- **11:30 (69 blocks) is the format floor, not a target.** It cannot be reached without
  dropping two of the five layers, at which point the structural form the slate assigned
  this chapter is gone and the episode is a recitation.

**16:00 is the recommendation.** It sits inside the requested window rather than at
either edge, it is where the usable material genuinely stops, and the thing that sets
the length here is not the source's length but the **shape of a nested frame with a
twenty-seven-item catalogue inside it**: five layers, each of which needs a dream
reported, an entry read, an unpacking and a wake, and no way to run three of them and
still arrive anywhere.

## What this episode argues

The obvious reading of this chapter is that ancient Chinese medicine had a system of
dream interpretation. That reading is wrong in the way that matters, it is the reading
that sells, and the slate names it as this chapter's one discipline: *"the catalogue
must not be offered as a diagnostic tool. Never phrase it as 'if you dream of falling,
your X is deficient.' Frame as: this is what a physician in the Han dynasty believed a
dream told him."*

**The spine is that this is the oldest surviving attempt to explain dreams without
letting them mean anything.** Four things happen in this chapter:

1. **A dream is given a physical cause and nothing else.** An excess enters, finds
   nowhere to lodge, travels with the two circulations, and unsettles the sleeper. The
   dream that follows is not a message, not a portent and not a wish. It is what a
   surplus or a shortfall looks like from inside.
2. **The catalogue then refuses every reading that was available to it.** Hunger
   produces dreams of taking and fullness produces dreams of giving — and the chapter
   calls the first neither greed nor theft. **Nothing in twenty-seven entries is about
   the character, the future, or the biography of the man asleep.** That is an absence,
   and an absence is easy to oversell and easy to state precisely, so the episode states
   it precisely.
3. **It is checkable in principle and nobody checked it.** The flying-and-falling pair
   makes a claim that could have been tested by anybody in two thousand years. That is
   what separates it from divination and it is also its indictment.
4. **And then it does the thing that makes it medicine.** Twelve fullnesses are to be
   drained and fifteen shortfalls supplemented, and the symptoms stop at once. A dream
   has become something a physician acts on. **The chapter is not filed under dreams at
   all — it is filed under a man who cannot lie still.**

**The thesis, stated once and hedged hard:** none of the twenty-seven correspondences is
true, there is no mechanism in the chapter and no quantity anywhere in it, and the one
entry a modern researcher would nod at is the one anybody would get right by accident.
What the chapter has instead is the right *kind* of answer — a dream as a readout of a
body rather than a message to a person — arrived at eighteen centuries before anybody
could say why. **The episode says that out loud at blocks 82 to 84 and again at 90, and
the reckoning is deliberately not saved for the end**: the midpoint carries its own at
block 52.

**The dramatic engine is the nested frame.** Fan-di is the patient this time, which
inverts this cast's usual arrangement: he is not interrogating the text, he is the
evidence for it. **He wakes five times and the first four are false.** Each false wake
closes one class of the catalogue, the lamp is lower, and Dr-Qi has not moved. **The
fan is the tell** — he has it in his hand in the waking world and never in a dream
layer, so for most of the episode he is undefended without knowing it. It goes down for
the last time at **block 92**.

**Lei-Gong is the auditor**, and his brief this episode is a single question: *is this a
theory or a list?* His nine blocks are findings — that nobody in the chapter is visited
by anything (15); that every entry is in the present tense and none of them foretells
(29); that the flying entry makes a claim that could have been checked and never was
(38); that China had an official dream-diviner and the most famous dream in its
literature, and this chapter mentions neither (49); that *flying* is owned twice, by two
different causes, and the chapter never notices (58); that the fifteen are ordered by an
anatomical route rather than by dream content and run five and five and five (78); that
the one entry it gets right is the one a stopped clock gets right (85); and the verdict
at 91.

**Every one of the 96 blocks below is inside its voice's range**, verified by parsing
this document's own narration table and dropping tokens containing no letter or digit —
because a spaced em-dash is not a word and counting it as one spends real budget on a
window 1.4s wide.

Four consequences shape every line here, and none is stylistic:

- **The short interjection is dead.** Under an 8.6s floor there is no quick jab, and
  `--clip-seconds` moves the window rather than widening it. **Every character line is a
  full paragraph** — including nine dream reports that would each be one sentence in a
  stage play. This is why Lei-Gong has nine blocks rather than thirty: at 47–52 words
  each, an auditor who comments on everything becomes the narrator.
- **No comma list of three or more items appears anywhere in this script, and there is
  not a single comma in any of the 96 narration cells.** The 2026-08-09 measurement put
  a 31-word Arthur line with seven commas at **12.022s** against **8.913s** for the same
  31 words with none — a 3.1s swing from punctuation alone, on a window 1.4s wide.
  **This chapter is a catalogue of twenty-seven items** and is therefore the worst
  possible case for that failure. Every enumeration below is joined with *and* or split
  into sentences. **Do not add one.**
- **The rates are survivor-biased.** ~4.4 w/s is what Arthur's *passing* takes
  delivered, and his rate moved between **3.98 and 4.80 w/s within a single seven-block
  cut** on lines of near-identical length. A word count predicts a take only to about
  ±1.5s. **Generate two variants per block and keep the better one** — at ~1.45 a take
  that is the cheapest reliable method.
- **Reach the window with words, not full stops.** The assembler warns on internal pauses
  ≥0.8s, so padding a thin line with sentence breaks passes the duration gate and trips
  the pause warning. **No block below exceeds four sentences.**

These are **arithmetic against measured rates, not measurements**. Treat the first pass
as calibration and budget re-takes explicitly — the preflight below does.

### How to read this script

`CLAUDE.md` asks for **SOUND / VISUAL / CHARACTER** blocks and a numbered shot list;
`build_subtitles.js` needs a **three-column narration table** to build the required
`.srt`/`.vtt` sidecars. They are combined rather than duplicated:

- **The narration tables are canonical for words.** Blocks run continuously **1–96
  across the whole episode**, never per-act; the assembly manifest is flat and act-local
  numbering produces off-by-one errors that are expensive to find in a sixteen-minute
  render.
- **The SOUND / VISUAL / direction prose between them is canonical for performance**,
  and the **numbered shot list after block 96 is canonical for picture.** Neither
  restates a narration line.
- **The speaker is named in the beat column**, not a fourth column — the parser reads
  exactly three, and a fourth would leak into the caption text.
- **No markdown inside a narration cell.** `build_subtitles.js` copies the third column
  into the cue verbatim and strips nothing, so a `**bold**` emphasis burns into the
  picture as literal asterisks. **Put emphasis in the direction prose, never in the
  line.**

> **Do not insert a table whose first cell is a bare integer anywhere between blocks 1
> and 96.** `parseNarration` keeps the first consecutive run from block 1 and stops at
> the first row that breaks it, so a stray numeric table silently truncates the sidecar.
> Every other table in this document sits before block 1 or after block 96 for that
> reason, and the ON-SCREEN TEXT card table prefixes its first column with `Block` so it
> cannot collide. The shot list after block 96 is a numbered *list*, not a table, for the
> same reason.

## The hall at night — the visual system

The slate assigns this chapter the **nested frame** form, and §2.2 makes clear why that
matters beyond taste: twenty episodes in the same Q&A shape is the Generic/Repetitive
Content failure mode, so **every episode carries a distinct structural form.** Here the
form is staged as depth, and the argument is staged as a direction.

**The whole episode plays in one room — a dark hall with a low sleeping mat at its
centre, one oil lamp beside the mat, and a folding screen of blank silk panels behind
it.**

| Act | The layer | The lamp, the screen and the camera |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | waking, and it is the only true waking until Act VII | lamp full; every panel blank; camera at mat level and still |
| Act I | still waking — the mechanism is explained before the first dream | lamp full; the first panel begins to darken from beneath |
| Act II — **wake one is false** | water and fire | lamp at three quarters; two panels inked; camera one step closer |
| Act III — **wake two is false** | above and below | lamp at half; four panels; one step closer |
| Act IV — **wake three is false** | hunger and fullness | lamp at a third; six panels; one step closer |
| Act V — **wake four is false** | the five organ entries | lamp guttering; eleven panels; one step closer, and the mat fills the frame |
| Act VI | the fifteen lodgings, run without a wake at all | lamp almost out; the screen fills with panels; closest position |
| Act VII — **wake five is true** | the reckoning | **lamp out, grey at the window, and the widest shot in the episode** |

Three rules keep it from becoming decoration:

- **Every dream image in this episode blooms upward through the silk from underneath,
  and nothing is ever painted onto it from above, in any of the ninety-six shots.** Not
  a brush, not a hand, not a beam of light, not a figure. **The loaded brush in shots 4
  and 30 and 61 comes down toward the silk and stops short and withdraws.** This is the
  episode's entire argument rendered as a rule about the picture: the dream comes from
  under, from the body, and not from above. A shot that breaks it says the opposite of
  its own narration.
- **No dream panel ever contains a face or a human figure.** The catalogue's dreams are
  water and fire and height and depth and hills and marshes and roads and towns, and it
  is a claim of this episode that none of them is about a person. The three entries where
  a person is unavoidable — mutual killing, the head struck off, and the ceremony of
  bowing — are carried by **two ink strokes that cross and cancel, an empty lacquer
  collar-stand, and a pair of shoes set together at a threshold.** See *Compliance notes*.
- **The register never changes.** Everything stays flat 2D ink-wash, and the four modern
  comparisons are carried by Arthur's voice over the same brushed idiom. **There is no
  photograph, no brain scan, no sleep laboratory, no swirling galaxy, no floating body
  and no third eye in ninety-six shots.** For this chapter those are the single largest
  prompt-stage risk, because each one imports the dream-decoder reading the episode
  spends three blocks refusing.

**One more rule, and it is a safety-filter rule rather than an artistic one: there is no
reclining or prone human figure anywhere in this episode.** Fan-di is **upright on the
mat in every shot he appears in**, including the dream layers. A chapter about sleep
invites a body lying down and the spleen entry invites a body pinned by weight, and
restraint or bound-figure imagery has returned `nsfw` from this service before on
innocuous subject matter. The weight in shot 61 is **a bronze weight resting on folded
silk**, not on anybody.

## Source text and translation

The chapter arrived as `05_Ch_LS43__The_Dream_Catalogue.md`, a ctext.org-style export
in **simplified Chinese** with an English rendering beneath each section. **Every
character below has been converted to traditional by hand and is not from the source

### §1 — the mechanism

> 黃帝曰：願聞淫邪泮衍，奈何？岐伯曰：正邪從外襲內，而未有定舍，反淫於藏，不得定處，與營衛俱行，而與魂魄飛揚，使人臥不得安而喜夢；氣淫於府，則有餘於外，不足於內；氣淫於藏，則有餘於內，不足於外。

**Working translation.** *The Yellow Emperor said: I should like to hear about the
overflowing evil and its spreading. How does it work?* — *Qi Bo said: An ordinary evil
presses from the outside inward, and it has no fixed lodging. Instead it overflows into
the depots, and it cannot settle there either. It travels together with the constructive
and the defensive qi, and it sets the hun and the po flying and scattering, so that a
man lies down and cannot rest and is full of dreams. When the qi overflows into the
palaces there is surplus without and shortfall within. When it overflows into the depots
there is surplus within and shortfall without.*

### §2 — the twelve fullnesses

> 黃帝曰：有餘不足，有形乎？岐伯曰：陰氣盛，則夢涉大水而恐懼；陽氣盛，則夢大火而燔焫；陰陽俱盛，則夢相殺。上盛則夢飛，下盛則夢墮；甚饑則夢取，甚飽則夢予；肝氣盛，則夢怒，肺氣盛，則夢恐懼、哭泣、飛揚；心氣盛，則夢善笑恐畏；脾氣盛，則夢歌樂，身體重不舉；腎氣盛，則夢腰脊兩解不屬。凡此十二盛者，至而寫之，立已。

**Working translation.** *The Yellow Emperor asked: Do surplus and shortfall take a
visible form?* — *Qi Bo said: When the yin qi is full, one dreams of wading a great
water and is afraid. When the yang qi is full, one dreams of a great fire burning
fiercely. When yin and yang are both full, one dreams of men killing one another. When
the upper part is full, one dreams of flying; when the lower part is full, one dreams of
falling. In extreme hunger one dreams of taking; in extreme fullness one dreams of
giving. When the liver qi is full, one dreams of anger. When the lung qi is full, one
dreams of fear and weeping and flying up. When the heart qi is full, one dreams of
laughing readily and of dread. When the spleen qi is full, one dreams of singing and
rejoicing, and the body is heavy and cannot be lifted. When the kidney qi is full, one
dreams that the waist and the spine have come apart and are no longer joined. All these
twelve fullnesses: when they arrive they are drained, and it stops at once.*

### §3 — the fifteen shortfalls

> 厥氣客於心，則夢見丘山煙火；客於肺，則夢飛揚，見金鐵之奇物；客於肝，則夢山林樹木；客於脾，則夢見丘陵大澤，壞屋風雨；客於腎，則夢臨淵，沒居水中；客於膀胱，則夢遊行；客於胃，則夢飲食；客於大腸，則夢田野；客於小腸，則夢聚邑衝衢；客於膽，則夢鬥訟自刳；客於陰器，則夢接內；客於項，則夢斬首；客於脛，則夢行走而不能前，及居深地窌苑中；客於股肱，則夢禮節拜起；客於胞䐈，則夢溲便。凡此十五不足者，至而補之立已也。

**Working translation.** *When the rebellious qi lodges as a guest in the heart, one
dreams of hills and mountains with smoke and fire. In the lung, of flying up and of
strange objects of metal and iron. In the liver, of mountain forests and trees. In the
spleen, of hills and great marshes and of ruined houses in wind and rain. In the kidney,
of standing at the edge of a deep pool, or of living submerged in water. In the bladder,
of travelling. In the stomach, of eating and drinking. In the large intestine, of open
fields. In the small intestine, of crowded towns and busy crossroads. In the
gallbladder, of quarrels and litigation* **[and of self-wounding — excluded, see below]**.
***In the genitals*** **[entry excluded entire, see below]**. *In the neck, of the head
being struck off. In the shins, of walking and being unable to go forward, or of dwelling
deep underground in a cellar or enclosure. In the thighs and arms, of ceremony and of
bowing and rising. In the lower abdomen, of passing water and stool. All these fifteen
shortfalls: when they arrive they are supplemented, and it stops at once.*

### Departures, exclusions and things to verify

Seven items, and each is argued rather than asserted.

1. **正邪 is treated as one technical term, not two.** The supplied English reads
   *"righteous and pathogenic influences,"* which parses 正 and 邪 as a pair. This script
   follows the commentarial reading in which 正邪 is a single term for an *ordinary* or
   *mild* evil — one that enters without a strong assault, as against 虛邪, the deviant
   evil that strikes a depleted body. **Blocks 10 and 11 are written to that reading.**
   It is the one philological judgement in the script that changes a line's meaning, and
   it should be checked against a reference edition before block 10's take is recorded.
2. **淫 is rendered as *overflowing*, and the episode says so out loud at block 11.**
   The supplied title reads *"Dreams Caused by Licentious and Pathogenic Influences."*
   In this book 淫 carries the sense of spreading past a bound, and *licentious* imports
   a sexual reading the chapter does not have. **This is a genuine trap for an English
   audience and block 11 exists to spring it deliberately** rather than to quietly
   correct it.
3. **寫 in 至而寫之 reads as 瀉, to drain.** The character in the received text is the
   older graph; it is not *to write*. Block 65 renders it as *drained*. Anyone reading
   the card at block 65 with a modern dictionary will get the wrong verb, which is why
   the working translation states it here.
4. **歧伯 in the source is 岐伯 in the standard text.** The supplied file uses the
   variant throughout. Nothing in the script or on any card carries the name — he is
   **Dr-Qi** — so nothing downstream depends on it, but it is recorded because it is a
   signal about the provenance of the export.
5. **胞䐈 is textually uncertain and the script renders it as *the lower belly*.**
   Commentators divide between reading 胞 as the bladder and as the uterus, and 䐈 is a
   rare graph. The bladder has already had its own entry at 膀胱 earlier in the same
   list, which is itself an argument that this last one is not simply the bladder again.
   **Block 77 says *lower belly* and block 79's incorporation claim is about bladder
   pressure**, which is a claim about the phenomenon rather than about the graph. If the
   reading is challenged, block 79 is the line to re-check.
6. **Two things are excluded on compliance grounds and neither is an oversight.** The
   entry 客於陰器，則夢接內 — a dream of sexual intercourse — appears in no narration
   line and no shot. The clause 自刳 in the gallbladder entry — a dream of wounding
   oneself — is likewise absent, while the rest of that entry (鬥訟, quarrels and
   litigation) is kept. **Block 75 states on screen that both have been left out**, so
   that a viewer counting to fifteen is not misled and so that nobody later restores
   them believing they were overlooked. They are quoted above only for the same reason.
7. **The count of twenty-seven is ours.** The chapter counts **twelve** and **fifteen**
   in its own two summary sentences and never adds them. Blocks 8, 19, 82 and 85 use the
   sum, which is arithmetic on the chapter's own numbers.

**One claim in the script is not from this chapter at all and is marked as ours**: block
13's statement that the defensive qi turns inward at nightfall. That is the canon's
doctrine and it is set out elsewhere in the Lingshu rather than here. It is the script's
bridge from a mechanism to a *night-time* mechanism, and it is phrased as *the canon has*
rather than as *this chapter says*. **Verify the attribution before block 13's take is
recorded.**

**Block 20's dating caveat is ours and is the line a specialist checks first.** The
received Lingshu reaches us through a Song-dynasty transmission and its dating is
genuinely argued over. Block 20 says exactly that and no more.

2. **Append the job IDs to this document immediately**, before moving on.
3. Assemble the act as its own `assemble_final.sh` run to check pacing early, exporting

**`background: true` is the decision to get right and it has already lost a run.** A
trailer-scale assembly finishes inside the 120s foreground budget; **an act of 12 to 16

the Suwen 1 v5 run `background: true` timed out the transport call, the sandbox was
reclaimed, and the finished render went with it. **If a background job is used here,
poll the returned log with `tail` at least every 60 seconds** — the sandbox dies under
an unpolled job.

**The join is the open problem and it must be decided before an episode's worth of takes
is generated, not at the join.** `assemble_final.sh` takes clip/voice pairs against a
declared block count and asserts an `N × 10s` output, so an assembled act is not a valid
input to it. Two routes, neither yet run here:

- **Assemble the whole episode in one call** and skip the join. Removes the problem and
  is the ~192-file download.
- **Concat the act outputs** with our own ffmpeg. Defensible — the acts are already
  assembled correctly and a concat of equal-geometry MP4s is a lossless remux — but it
  puts the −16 LUFS normalisation per act rather than across the episode. **Check levels
  at the seven seams**, and note that this episode's seams are unusually exposed: Acts
  IV and VII are scored dry on purpose, so a level step at 43 or 81 will be audible.

Flags for every act run: `--out`, `--blocks <act count>`, `--manifest pairs.txt`. No
`--clip-seconds` (leave the window alone). No `--music` until guqin licensing is cleared.
**No `--subs`** — it is a hard error.

continuous numbering rather than act-local numbering.** The script cross-checks the
numbers in each pair; act-local numbering is how audio ends up on the wrong block.

fixed.
