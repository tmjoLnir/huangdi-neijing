## Shot list

1. **The question at dawn** — a high stone terrace at sunrise; Fan-di at the rail taps his closed fan against his palm on a question he can't put down, Dr-Qi waits behind him with her open bamboo scroll, and a single faint gold thread of light rises from his chest toward the sun. (Maps to Cold Open / Act I.)
2. **The landscape that is a body** — Dr-Qi draws in the air with a bamboo slip; ink lines bloom upward into a map of the nine regions, then the map folds inward and becomes a standing human silhouette with weather moving through it and a gold glow gathering at its edge. (Act I: the one current, the body as landscape.)
3. **Yang qi is your sun / the four thieves** — night courtyard, Dr-Qi lifts a paper lamp before a wall-painted gold sun disc; the tall frame then splits into four stacked weather bands with Lei-Gong as the demonstrator in each: frost, white heat shimmer, grey rain, hard wind. (Act II: the sun analogy and the Four Thieves.)
4. **Self-inflicted injury** — the emperor's study at 3 a.m., lamps and scattered documents, Fan-di dishevelled with spectacles pushed up; the lamp flame flares tall and thin then gutters; an ink-wash inset of a city wall breaching under floodwater; then Fan-di by day, fan snapped shut in a white-knuckled fist, the gold light around him draining to cold blue. (Act III: 煎厥 / 薄厥, kept atmospheric and non-graphic.)
5. **The clock inside the day** — vertical split: above, a time-lapse sky from dawn to dusk with a sundial's shadow sweeping; below, a stylized Lei-Gong whose inner gold light tracks it exactly, peaking at noon and banking low as he finally lies down to sleep while Dr-Qi slides a screen closed. (Act IV: the daily rhythm — "go to bed.")
6. **The fourteen characters / title card** — dawn on the same terrace, mirroring the open; all three stand still as the sun clears the mountains and the same light moves inside their outlines; the frame settles into a series title-card layout, gold sun disc arced above, clean lower third held for the title overlay. (Close: 阴平阳秘，精神乃治.)

Subtitles/CC are burned in server-side at assembly (font **anton**), per the
project's compliance rule to always caption the deliverable; the clips
themselves stay text-free.

## Compliance notes (YouTube)

- **Medical content** — philosophy/history framing only, no treatment named and no
  substance named; the not-medical-advice disclaimer is carried as the blockquote
  above and goes at the top of the video description. The script's honesty
  mechanism is preserved: the narrator carries the hedge ("Not astrology.
  Something simpler"), never a claim.
- **Mortality / collapse** — the two collapse passages (煎厥 *jiān jué*, 薄厥 *báo
  jué*) are represented only atmospherically in block 4: a lamp flame flaring
  and guttering, and an ink-wash city wall under floodwater. No bodies fall, no
  injury, no gore — "portraits, not bodies." The script's on-screen emergency-care
  card belongs to the full episode; the trailer avoids the passage's clinical
  content entirely rather than half-stating it.
- **Indulgence** — the chapter's rich-table material is not used in the trailer;
  no feast, no bottles, no pouring, no drinking.
- **Supernatural hook** — "a body wired into the sky" (block 1) is explicitly
  debunked in block 2 ("Not astrology") and paid off educationally in blocks 5–6
  (daily rhythm; yin level and yang sealed). The title honors the payoff.
- **Audience** — general audiences; self-certify **not** "made for kids."
