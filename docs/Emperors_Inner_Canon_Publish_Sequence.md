# The Emperor's Inner Canon — Publish Sequence

*Roll-out strategy for the 20-episode slate. Tier-1 (US/UK/CA/AU) audience.*

---

## The core principle

**Growth-first, not syllabus-first.** A brand-new channel's only job in month one is to get the algorithm to notice it and convert cold viewers into subscribers. So you order episodes for *reach and monetization*, not in the order a teacher would explain the material. You can't teach an audience you don't have yet — teach the foundations once subscribers exist to watch them.

**Cadence.** Batch all of Phase 1 *before* you launch, so you can publish weekly through the make-or-break first five weeks, then settle into a sustainable biweekly pace (realistic for 15–20 min animation).

## The one call to get right: publish your foundations LATE

Yin-Yang (Ch 5) and Yang Qi (Ch 3) are the concepts every other episode rests on — so instinct says open with them. **Override that instinct.**

1. **Why not first** — on a brand-new channel a $5–8 "101" explainer published cold has no audience and simply dies in the algorithm.
2. **The fix** — seed the ~20 seconds of context a viewer needs inside the first 30 seconds of each earlier episode that requires it. Don't make them watch a separate lesson.
3. **Why Ep 13** — by then you have subscribers who will search for, watch and binge it. It becomes the evergreen hub video every other episode links back to.

*If you're optimizing for a coherent course rather than channel growth, this call flips — and Ch 5 opens the series.*

## Playlists ≠ publish order

Publish for reach; group for binge. Six clusters carry session watch-time no matter when each episode drops.

- **Longevity** — 01 · 40 · 68 — live-long promise → who's built for it → the whole arc of a life.
- **The Mind** — 42 · 35 · 19 — five spirits → dreams → how emotions move the body.
- **Daily Rhythm** — 46 · 61 · 02 — sleep → worse-at-night → living by the seasons.
- **Foundations** — 05 · 03 · 06 — yin-yang OS → yang qi → the body as an empire.
- **Diagnosis** — 09 · 57 · 18 — pulse → face → the tell-tale cough.
- **Disease & Danger** — 21 · 25 · 15 · 28 — the higher-risk cluster; gate behind a mature, established channel.
